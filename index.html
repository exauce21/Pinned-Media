Our perspective

Real stories. Real people.

Pinned Media tells real stories with real people. Rooted in journalism, we create unscripted cinematic documentaries, photography and interactive storytelling that centre underrepresented communities, Africa and its diaspora.

We connect local creatives across Africa and the diaspora to support organisations, brands and publishers with powerful stories and effective communications. Working in French and English, we bring cultural insight, international experience and local knowledge to projects across the continent.

How we work

01

Brief

Engage us for a production project, a communications campaign or a monthly agency partnership. We agree your objectives, audience, budget, scope and approval process, with clear deliverables and measures of success.

02

Plan

We research the story, listen to contributors and develop the editorial and visual approach. We connect the right local creatives, coordinate locations and plan a shared schedule for productions across one or several countries.

03

Produce

Local filmmakers and creatives bring their knowledge, relationships and perspective to the production. We coordinate teams across countries simultaneously, capturing real people and unfolding stories with cultural nuance and care.

04

Deliver

We deliver content and manage publishing across agreed channels. For monthly agency partnerships, we review performance and refine the communications plan with you.

Meet the team

These are the four people you’re most likely to meet when you first get in touch. Behind them is an extensive team of filmmakers, producers, designers and local creatives across Africa and the diaspora, brought together around each project.

  • Benedicte Kalombo

    Founder and Executive Producer

    Benedicte is a Congolese-born multimedia journalist and producer whose experience includes the BBC, ITN/ITV News, The Independent and New African Woman. Through Pinned Media, Pin Africa and Acquire by Pin Africa, she champions African and underrepresented voices. Her journalistic approach shapes our commitment to real people, cultural nuance and powerful visual storytelling.

  • Ayo Sanusi

    Producer and Cinematographer

    Ayo combines cinematography, producing and story development to create films that connect emotionally with audiences. His individual credits include work with the Premier League and DAZN as a cinematographer and producer, alongside experience on the ground at the FIFA World Cup in Qatar. He brings this visual craft and understanding of football culture to our unscripted storytelling.

  • Letitia Kamayi

    Production Manager and Technical Lead

    A photographer with a precise eye for lighting, Letitia manages production on the ground and coordinates the technical setup. Her knowledge of cameras and equipment helps ensure every crew has the tools and support to bring the story to life.

  • Janette Daramy

    Production Operations Manager

    Janette keeps productions organised behind the scenes, developing budgets, tracking projects and coordinating locations, personnel and client communication. She brings the planning and oversight that help our teams deliver smoothly across locations.
